<objective>
Route every Open Original entry point through one `useOpenOriginal` helper (D-10). The helper opens the tab synchronously first. It then fires a bodyless `PUT /api/articles/{id}/engagement/open`, whose errors are swallowed silently (D-12), with no client dedupe (D-11). On success it refreshes only the exact by-id article query (D-06). In-body links and Copy Link stay unreported (CAPT-07).

Purpose: this is the client half of CAPT-01. The tab must always open, even with the server down, and the open must be reported once per call through one path.
Output: an optional-body `apiPut`, `articlesApi.recordOpen`, the `EngagementKind` client type, `hooks/useEngagement.ts` with its test, and the ReadingPane buttons and `o` shortcut wired through the helper.
</objective>
