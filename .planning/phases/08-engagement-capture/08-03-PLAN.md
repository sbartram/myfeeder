<objective>
Capture saves server-side in the services that own them. STAR is recorded in `ArticleService.updateState`, only on an unstarred→starred change (CAPT-02). BOARD is recorded in `BoardService.addArticle` (CAPT-03, D-09). RAINDROP is recorded in `RaindropService`, only after `createBookmark` returns (CAPT-04). All three are best-effort through `recordQuietly`, outside any transaction (D-07, D-08). The plan then proves that engagement is sticky and cascades safely (CAPT-05), that nothing else records it (CAPT-07), and that the ranking is unchanged (SC-5). It also pins the V7 shape with a migration test and documents the phase in CLAUDE.md.

Purpose: saves have exactly one server path each, so capturing them there covers every client entry point (★ and s, 📋/🔖/b/Read Later, 💧 and v) and can never count a failed Raindrop save.
Output: three one-line capture calls with their unit tests, integration proofs, V7EngagementMigrationTest and the CLAUDE.md update.
</objective>
