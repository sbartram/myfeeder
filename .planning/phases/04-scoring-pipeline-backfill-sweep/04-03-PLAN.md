<objective>
Build the scorer: judge one article with the shared builders in one Jev call, store the raw outputs write-once, and classify every failure as permanent (FAILED, attempt used) or transient (nothing written). Fold in the CR-01 truncate fix (D-13), because the background scorer sends exactly that state.

Purpose: this is SCOR-01's "judged once in a single call" and SCOR-07's attempt rules at the service level. The classifier decides what the user pays for: a wrong "transient" re-bills every sweep forever, and a wrong "permanent" burns attempts during an outage.

Output: `ArticleScoringService`, `ScoringFailure`, a Postgres-backed flow test, a Mockito unit test, and the truncate fix with CJK/URL tests.
</objective>
