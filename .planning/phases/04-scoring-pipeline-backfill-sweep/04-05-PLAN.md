<objective>
Add the one drain loop: a two-minute sweep that finds eligible unscored articles, newest first, and feeds them to the scoring queue whenever Jev is configured, the rubric is not empty and the breaker is not open. Then prove the full pipeline end to end with a live key at the end of the phase.

Purpose: SCOR-05. The launch backfill, outage recovery, a late-added key, first scoring after the profile is written, and the Re-score drain are all this one job, because "needs scoring" lives in the database (R3). The batch cap (D-16) keeps fresh arrivals from waiting behind up to 1,000 backlog items on the single FIFO thread.

Output: `InterestScoringSweep`, its test, the `myfeeder.interest` YAML blocks, a context proof of the schedule, and the end-of-phase human check.
</objective>
