<objective>
Put the interest badge on every article response and give `GET /api/articles/{id}` an exact "Why N?" breakdown whose rows add up to the badge.

Purpose: D-18 makes `interestScore` a contract on every article response (rated costly: ArticleList, BoardArticleList, ReadingPane and Phase 6 all consume it). PRIO-04 and D-02 require a breakdown whose integer rows sum exactly to `round(raw)`, which research Pattern 4 does server-side with a pure largest-remainder helper against the SQL total. Both reuse plan 05-01's blend CTE, so badge, sort and breakdown cannot disagree.

Output: `displayScores`, `breakdownInputs` and their records on `InterestScoreQueries`; `ScoreBreakdowns`; `InterestBreakdown`; `Article.interestBreakdown`; enrichment in `ArticleService` and `BoardService`; `findByIdWithBreakdown`; tests.
</objective>
