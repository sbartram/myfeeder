<objective>
Connect ingest to the scorer without ever touching polling: the poll publishes the ids it inserted, a never-throwing listener filters them through the shared predicate and hands them to a dedicated one-thread bounded executor, and the scorer runs there.

Purpose: SCOR-02 and success criterion 2. `pollFeed` has no transaction, so an after-commit listener runs inline on the single polling thread, and any exception it throws would reach the catch that increments `feed.errorCount` (research Pattern 1). A plain executor bean would also silently remove Boot's `applicationTaskExecutor` (Pitfall 1), a queue that grows past core would spawn threads (Pitfall 2), and a discarding rejection policy would leak in-flight ids so the sweep could never retry them (Pitfall 3).

Output: event record, listener, executor config, `ScoringQueue`, the poll-time publish, and the isolation, queue and context tests.
</objective>
