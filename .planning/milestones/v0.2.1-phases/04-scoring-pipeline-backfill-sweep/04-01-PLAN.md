<objective>
Harden the Jev client for the background scorer before any scorer code exists: make the breaker count articles instead of attempts (D-07), give multi-topic calls room (D-05, D-06), let a paused breaker recover on its own (D-17), and keep caller bugs and unsupported question types out of both the bill and the breaker (D-14, D-15). Then make the CLAUDE.md convention describe what actually runs.

Purpose: every scorer and sweep test in later plans assumes these semantics. With Retry outermost, 4 failing articles open the breaker; without auto-transition, a sweep that skips while OPEN never makes the call that would move the breaker to HALF_OPEN, so scoring could stay paused indefinitely (research Pitfall 5). SCOR-07's "transient failures don't consume attempts" relies on the breaker opening at the intended rate, and SCOR-05's "recovery after an outage" relies on the automatic transition.

Output: YAML changes in both files, one client check, rewritten and new resilience tests, a TypeSafeConfigTest update, a hunk-only CLAUDE.md commit and a narrowed pending todo.
</objective>
