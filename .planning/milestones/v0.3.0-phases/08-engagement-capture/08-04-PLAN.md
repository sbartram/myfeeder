<objective>
Show the Forget-engagement control (CAPT-06, D-01..D-06). The reading pane's score row gains a small `Engaged: opened, starred · Forget` line whenever the open article has engagement. The line also appears on engaged but unscored articles, which is the common case. Clicking Forget deletes at once and the line disappears after the by-id refetch. The Raindrop, board and Read Later saves also refresh the by-id article (exact query only), so the line updates as soon as a save is recorded.

Output: `articlesApi.forgetEngagement`, `useForgetEngagement`, the engagement line in `ScoreRow` with its CSS and a new component test, a ReadingPane mock update, and the exact by-id invalidation in three save hooks with a new test.
</objective>
