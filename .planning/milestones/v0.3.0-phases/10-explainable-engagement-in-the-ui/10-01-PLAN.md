<objective>
Give every engagement action taken in Priority the same in-place reaction a vote has (LRN-06, SC-2, D-05..D-08). One shared, never-rejecting `afterEngagement(qc, id, onPriority)` refetches the article by id with `staleTime: 0`, invalidates the vote's refresh set, and on Priority patches the row's score and lights "Ranking changed" only when the score actually changed. Task 1 (tracer) wires the open end to end and proves it on the real PriorityList. Task 2 wires star and Forget. Task 3 wires Raindrop, board add and Read Later, and moves the vote hook onto the shared invalidation set.

Purpose: engaging never reshuffles the list being triaged, while the badge, "Why N?" and the other lists catch up at once.
Output: `hooks/engagementReaction.ts` with its unit test; six hooks wired; the Phase 8 "by-id only" refresh tests rewritten to the D-07 set; a PriorityList component case.
</objective>
