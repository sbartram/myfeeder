- [myfeeder OpenWiki Quickstart](quickstart.md) - Entry point for the myfeeder codebase wiki — a Spring Boot 4 / React feed reader (Feedly-style RSS/Atom/JSON Feed aggregator) that scores every article's personal interest via a Jev (TypeSafe) rubric, learns from thumbs feedback and engagement, surfaces topic-suggestion gaps, and exports saved articles to Raindrop.io.
